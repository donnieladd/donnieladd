## Building systems, not just software.

I’m a solutions architect and builder focused on designing environments where **people, processes, data, software, and intelligence work as one system**.

That means my work often begins before the first line of code.

I care about:

- the problem underneath the feature
- the architecture underneath the interface
- the workflow around the software
- the data moving between systems
- the decisions AI should — and should not — make
- the human experience inside the technology
- and whether the system can actually survive real-world complexity

My work spans **product strategy, software architecture, AI systems, agentic workflows, automation, integrations, operational design, and implementation.**

> **People · Process · Data · Software · Intelligence**

That intersection is where I do my best work.

## The Work

Most of what interests me isn’t a single app.

It’s the system around it.

I build across layers:

```text
01  Understand the problem
        ↓
02  Model the organization + workflow
        ↓
03  Design the system architecture
        ↓
04  Connect data + services
        ↓
05  Build the software
        ↓
06  Add intelligence + agency
        ↓
07  Automate execution
        ↓
08  Design human control + governance
        ↓
09  Observe, learn + improve
```

The objective is not maximum automation.

It is **better orchestration of human and machine capability.**

## Current Research & Build Focus

### AI-native architecture

What changes when intelligence is designed into the system from the beginning rather than attached to an existing product afterward?

### Agentic systems

How should agents reason, route work, use tools, collaborate, maintain context, escalate decisions, and operate under explicit authority?

### Allen

How do you move from orchestrating frontier models to building an intelligence platform with its own durable reasoning architecture, learning systems, tool environment, model strategy, safety boundaries, and eventually its own LLM capability?

### Human + AI systems

Where should humans remain directly involved? Where should software execute automatically? Where should AI assist rather than act?

### Operational intelligence

How can software understand not only information, but the organization, workflow, policies, goals, responsibilities, dependencies, and decisions surrounding that information?

## A Principle I Keep Coming Back To

> **Technology should disappear into capability.**

The best system is not the one with the most AI, the most automation, or the most complicated architecture.

It is the one that gives people **greater clarity, stronger judgment, more leverage, and less unnecessary work**.

Software should reduce complexity.

AI should increase human capacity.

Automation should remove work that no longer requires a person.

And architecture should make the whole thing hold together.

That’s the kind of technology I’m interested in building.
